# Qubo audio sources

All bundled one-shot effects are CC0 1.0.

- `spin.ogg`: “Whoosh low” by MeanRaccoon, Freesound 830856, CC0 1.0. Source: https://freesound.org/people/MeanRaccoon/sounds/830856/
- `wood-2.ogg`: wooden door-close foley from Kenney’s RPG Audio pack, CC0 1.0. Source pack: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio

All 30 environments now select a real CC0 field recording. Recordings are selected by closest theme and reused across environments; they stream on demand rather than being bundled locally:
- Rain: “Soft rain.WAV” by barkenov, Freesound 640655. https://freesound.org/people/barkenov/sounds/640655/
- Forest: “250418_10h28 Gergueil West ORTF” by Sadiquecat, Freesound 799439. https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/799439/
- Sea / waves: recording by SamsterBirdies, Freesound 578524. https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/578524/
- Wind: “breeze.wav” by keweldog, Freesound 181801. https://freesound.org/people/keweldog/sounds/181801/
- Stream: “Small brook, stream, water sound” by SamsterBirdies, Freesound 584269. https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/584269/
- River: “WATRFlow Outdoor” by JW_Audio, Freesound 810231. https://freesound.org/people/JW_Audio/sounds/810231/
- Cat purr: “Cat purr.wav” by Trautwein, Freesound 260881. https://freesound.org/people/Trautwein/sounds/260881/
- Low indoor hum: “ambient low hum (aircon)” by TimBahrij, Freesound 234918. https://freesound.org/people/TimBahrij/sounds/234918/

If a stream is unavailable or the device is offline, Qubo falls back to its built-in quiet scene soundscape.
